# ArChess Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ArChess ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our website and application (the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as:
- Username or display name
- Email address (for account creation and communication)
- Profile information (avatar, bio, preferences)

### Usage Information
We automatically collect certain information when you interact with the Service:
- IP address, browser type, and operating system
- Pages visited, features used, and time spent on each page
- Gameplay statistics (matches played, win/loss ratio, etc.)
- Device identifiers and advertising identifiers (where permitted)
- Referral URLs and search terms used to find our Service

### Cookies and Tracking Technologies
We use cookies and similar technologies to:
- Remember your preferences and settings
- Keep you logged into your account
- Understand how you use our Service
- Deliver personalized content and advertisements
- Prevent fraudulent activity and improve security

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve our Service
- Process transactions and send you related information (including purchase confirmations and invoices)
- Communicate with you about promotions, upcoming events, and other news
- Personalize your experience and show you relevant content
- Monitor and analyze usage trends to improve our Service
- Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or other harmful activities
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:
- **Service Providers**: Third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf (hosting, analytics, etc.)
- **Business Partners**: For joint offerings or promotional activities (with your consent where required)
- **Legal Authorities**: When required by law or to protect our rights or safety
- **Business Transfers**: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
- **Aggregated or Anonymized Data**: For research, marketing, or public reporting purposes

## 4. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Restrict or object to certain uses of your information
- Receive a copy of your information in a portable format

- Opt-out of direct marketing communications

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@archess.game

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including:
- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption of sensitive data at rest
- Regular security assessments and penetration testing
- Access controls and authentication mechanisms
- Secure backup and disaster recovery procedures
- Employee training on data protection and privacy

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Factors we consider include:
- The nature and sensitivity of the information
- The purposes for which we process the information
- Any legal or contractual obligations to retain the information

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

## 8. International Data Transfers

If you are located outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored on, and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers are located. By using our Service, you consent to such transfers.

## California Residents - Additional Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to:
- Know what personal information we collect about you
- Delete personal information we have collected about you
- Opt-out of the sale of your personal information
- Receive equal service and price, even if you exercise your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@archess.game with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:
- The right to be informed

- The right to rectification
- The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- The right to restrict processing
- The right to data portability
- The right to object
- Rights related to automated decision making and profiling

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@archess.game with "GDPR Request" in the subject line.
