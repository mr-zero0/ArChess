HOW TO PLAY

Launch. Collide. Survive.

ArChess starts like chess and immediately stops behaving like chess. Select one of the current player’s living pieces, pull backward, and release. Every piece becomes a physical projectile.

01Drag backward to aim and charge power.
02Release to launch opposite the drag.
03Impacts damage both pieces, including allies.
04Reduce the enemy King to 0 HP to win.
There is no check, checkmate, capture movement, castling, en passant, or piece-specific movement after launch. Physics gets the final vote.
