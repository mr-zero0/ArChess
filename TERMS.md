# ArChess Community & Match Rules

ArChess is a competitive physics-chess game. Players must not exploit client-side state, automate play, abuse matchmaking, impersonate other users, or intentionally disrupt matches.

Ranked results may be assigned for normal King destruction, surrender, timeout and abandonment. Deliberate abuse of disconnects or matchmaking may be penalized when moderation and authenticated accounts are enabled.

Cosmetics and progression must never change competitive piece statistics or physics parameters.
